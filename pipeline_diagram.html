🌾 RiceGuard AI Pipeline

All models share a single YOLO-based gatekeeper for rice leaf detection. The image must pass through Step 1 before any disease classification begins.

📸

Image Upload

User captures or uploads a photo of a rice leaf from the mobile app

1

Rice Leaf Detection

YOLO Gatekeeper — shared by ALL models

rice_leaf_detector.pt

The uploaded image is analyzed by the YOLOv8 rice leaf detector model to determine if it contains a rice leaf. This model was specifically trained to recognize rice leaves and reject non-leaf images (fingers, sky, random objects, etc.).

If the model is a detection model, it also crops the leaf region from the image, giving the downstream classifiers a cleaner input for better accuracy.
